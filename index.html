Clinician selection is required. This pack contains adult medicine examples and conditional alternatives, not patient-specific orders. Final doses, formulations, durations and quantities remain blank until entered by the prescriber. Emergency and review-only entries are separate workflows. No patient identifiers from the samples are included.

Original duplicate 18 preserved as 18A (AF CVR) and 18B (PSVT). All other serial numbers retained.

Platform rules

  • Final prescription requires clinician selection and completed patient-specific medicine fields; reference doses never autopopulate signed orders.
  • Unknown allergy status or known relevant allergy blocks affected agent pending review.
  • Require current renal/K data when dose or eligibility depends on them; apply exact local product label.
  • Pregnancy/lactation pathway must be reviewed; block ACEi/ARB/ARNI during pregnancy and review all other agents individually.
  • Expand every fixed-dose product into ingredient-level doses; detect cumulative metformin, duplicate SU/DPP4/SGLT2/statin/beta-blocker/LABA and duplicate insulin components.
  • No ACEi + ARB, ACEi + ARNI, or ARNI + additional ARB; enforce ACEi/ARNI washout.
  • Do not combine DPP-4 inhibitor with GLP-1 receptor agonist.
  • Do not combine finerenone with steroidal MRA; apply K/eGFR monitoring.
  • Anticoagulant + antiplatelet or DAPT requires indication, bleeding assessment and explicit review/stop plan.
  • Emergency/hospital-only templates cannot become routine outpatient take-home prescriptions; review-only options cannot emit drug orders.
  • Acute drugs need course/stop date; chronic drugs need ongoing flag, supply and review date. Supply duration is not treatment discontinuation.
  • Require units written in full, formulation/concentration/device, meal timing, glucose monitoring, titration and hypoglycaemia plan. No default sample doses or automatic conversion.
  • When merging diagnoses, consolidate identical ingredients only after checking dose/formulation/indication; never add doses from separate templates or silently choose one.
  • Enforce template mutually_exclusive_therapy_group_sets: choose monotherapy versus dual or dual versus triple branch, never both.
  • Use maintained formulary/interaction database: these curated checks are not a complete interaction or contraindication engine.

Sample reconciliation

  • Patient names, contact details, MRNs, visit IDs and hospital registration details were excluded.
  • Samples were used for the column structure and familiar medicine choices; they are not validation of the sampled regimen.
  • Combination strengths must be recorded per component. A displayed single 40 mg, 25 mg or 10 mg field does not describe a multidrug tablet.
  • The handwritten sample has unresolved brand/strength/frequency entries. These were not converted into platform defaults.
  • Repeated Ryzodeg doses and high insulin doses in the handwritten sample are patient-specific, not starter templates.
  • Clarithromycin in a sample is not a default bronchitis/URI antibiotic; QT and interactions plus actual bacterial diagnosis require review.
  • Montelukast/antihistamine, acebrophylline/mucolytic combinations and cough syrups are not universal asthma/COPD maintenance choices.
  • Trimetazidine/nicorandil, sedative/neuropathy medicines, vitamin supplements, urinary drugs and PPIs were not added without their own indication.
  • Some screenshot brand-to-generic descriptions appear inconsistent (including an oral gel); reconcile exact pack/formulary before import.
  • The import includes no guessed mapping for unclear products or unverified multidrug brands.
